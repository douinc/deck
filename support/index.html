Mac not
showing up?

Your iPhone lists your Mac a few seconds after both apps are open. If it keeps looking, check these in order.

  1. Deck is open on your Mac

    Deck needs its free Mac app. It sits at the top-right of your Mac's screen, in the menu bar.

    Click the Deck icon. It should say Waiting for your iPhone… If it says Deck is paused, choose Resume Deck.

    Don't have it yet? Install the Mac app.

  2. Same Wi‑Fi on both

    Your iPhone and Mac must be on the same network.

    Hotel, school, office and guest Wi‑Fi often stop devices from seeing each other. Turn on Personal Hotspot on your iPhone and join it from your Mac. Deck itself needs no internet.

  3. Local Network is allowed

    Both devices ask once for permission to find devices on your network. If either was declined, they can't see each other.

    On your iPhoneSettings → Deck → Local Network

    On your Mac (macOS 15 or later)System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Deck

  4. VPN is off

    A VPN on either device can hide them from each other.

    Turn it off on both while you present. Then quit Deck on your Mac (Deck icon → Quit Deck) and open it again.

Connecting

Do I need to install something on my Mac?

Yes. Deck on your iPhone talks to a small free app on your Mac, which presses the keys for you. Install it with Homebrew (brew tap douinc/tap, then brew install --cask deck) or download the DMG.

Deck needs a Mac running macOS 14 or later, an iPhone on iOS 18 or later, and watchOS 10 or later for Apple Watch. There is no Windows version.

It found my Mac, but says “Couldn't connect”
  1. Look at your Mac. If it asks to let Deck find devices on your network, click Allow.
  2. On your Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on Deck.
  3. Quit Deck on your Mac (Deck icon → Quit Deck), open it again, then tap Try Again on your iPhone.
The iPhone says “Deck can't look for your Mac”

Your iPhone is blocking Deck from your Wi‑Fi network. Tap Open Settings on that screen, turn on Local Network, and come back to Deck. It finds your Mac by itself.

Does Deck need the internet?

No. Your iPhone connects straight to your Mac over your own Wi‑Fi or your iPhone's Personal Hotspot. It works with no internet at all.

The connection dropped during my talk

Deck reconnects to the same Mac by itself, usually within a few seconds. Keep Deck open on your iPhone: if you lock the phone or switch apps, iOS pauses Deck and the connection ends until you open it again. The screen stays awake on its own while the remote is showing.

If it keeps dropping on venue Wi‑Fi, switch both devices to your iPhone's Personal Hotspot.

Slides and pointer

I'm connected, but the slides don't change
  1. On your Mac, click the Deck icon in the menu bar. If it says Deck can't change slides yet, click Fix: Allow Deck in System Settings… and turn on the switch next to Deck. Deck reopens by itself.
  2. Start your slideshow and click on it once, so it is the app in front. Deck controls whichever app is in front.
  3. If the switch was already on, turn it off and on again, then quit and reopen Deck.
Which apps does Deck work with?

Keynote, PowerPoint, Google Slides, Figma, PDF viewers, and any other Mac app that moves with the arrow keys. Deck presses the right arrow for Next and the left arrow for Previous.

My slides advance on a click, not an arrow key

Open settings on the remote (the gear button) and turn on Next Button Left-Clicks. Next then clicks wherever the Mac's cursor is, so leave the cursor over your slides.

How does the pointer work?

Hold the pointer button and aim your iPhone like a laser pointer to move the Mac's cursor. Tap the button to click.

For a spotlight that dims everything but your point, turn on Spotlight in the remote's settings. Pointer Speed is in the same place.

If the cursor doesn't move, update Deck on your Mac to version 2.0 or later and check that Deck is allowed under Accessibility.

Why does the Mac app ask for Accessibility?

Your Mac asks for your OK before any app can press keys or move the cursor for you. Deck uses that permission for three things: pressing the arrow keys, moving the cursor while you hold the pointer button, and clicking when you tap it.

Deck does not record what you type or read what is on your screen. The source code is on GitHub.

Remote and timer

How do I set a time limit?

With the timer stopped, slide your thumb along the timer arc above the buttons. It moves a minute at a time, up to 30 minutes. Slide all the way down for no limit. Then press play.

With a limit, the timer counts down and then shows how far over you are. Without one, it counts up.

What do the colors and buzzes mean?
MintOn track
AmberThree quarters of your time is gone
OrangeThe last tenth
CoralOver time
One buzzYour chosen interval has passed (every minute unless you change it)
Three buzzesHalftime
One long buzzTime's up
Two sharp buzzesEvery 30 seconds over time

Change the interval or turn buzzes off under Haptic Alerts in the remote's settings.

How do I move the buttons or switch hands?

Touch and hold any button except the pointer until the buttons wobble. Drag each one to where your thumb wants it, then tap Done. Reset puts them back.

Left-handed? Choose Left under Holding Hand in the remote's settings to flip the whole layout.

I can't move the buttons or change the limit

Both are locked while the timer is running, so a resting thumb can't change anything mid-talk. Pause the timer first.

Can I see the timer without opening the app?

Yes. While you are connected, the timer shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. If it doesn't, turn on Live Activities under Settings → Deck on your iPhone.

Apple Watch

How do I get Deck on my Apple Watch?

It comes with the iPhone app. If it isn't on your watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone, go to My Watch, and install Deck from the list of available apps.

The watch says “Connect your iPhone first” or “Disconnected”

Your watch reaches your Mac through your iPhone. Open Deck on your iPhone, tap your Mac's name, and keep Deck open there. The controls appear on your watch by themselves.

What are the ways to change slides from my wrist?
TapThe large button is Next. The small arrow is Previous.
Double tapTap your index finger and thumb together twice for Next.
Wrist flickFlick forward for Next, backward for Previous. Turn it on under Gesture Mode in the watch app's settings.
Digital CrownOne click of the Crown moves one slide.

Tap the time to start or pause the watch's timer. Press and hold it to reset.

Double tap doesn't change the slide
  • Double tap needs Apple Watch Series 9 or later, or Ultra 2, on watchOS 11 or later.
  • The display must be awake, so raise your wrist first.
  • In Wrist Flick mode, double tap turns flicks on and off instead.
Wrist flicks skip slides, go backward, or only go forward

Open settings in the watch app while in Wrist Flick mode:

Gesture LockWaits 3 seconds between flicks so you don't skip slides by accident
No Going BackOnly moves forward. Turn it off if Previous has stopped working.
Invert GesturesSwaps which direction is Next
Auto-toggle with WristFlicks turn on when you raise your wrist and off when you lower it

Subscription

What does Deck cost?

The iPhone app is free for 7 days, then $19.99 a year. That includes the Apple Watch app. The Mac app is free.

I subscribed, but Deck still asks me to

Tap Restore Purchases on that screen. Check that your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Deck. Apple handles billing, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I reinstalled Deck and my trial didn't start again

The trial runs for 7 days from the first time Deck opens on your iPhone. Deleting and reinstalling the app does not restart it.

Mac app and privacy

How do I update the Mac app?

When a newer version exists, the Deck menu shows Update Available. Click it to download, or run brew upgrade --cask deck.

Homebrew says the tap is untrusted

Run brew trust douinc/tap once, then install again. If you installed Deck from the old tap, run brew untap douinc/deck and brew tap douinc/tap.

Can Deck open when my Mac starts?

Yes. Click the Deck icon, choose Settings… and turn on Launch at Login.

How do I see the setup guide again?

Click the Deck icon in the menu bar and choose Setup Guide…

How do I uninstall Deck from my Mac?

Quit Deck from its menu, then run brew uninstall --cask deck or drag Deck from Applications to the Trash. To remove the permission as well, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and remove Deck.

What data does Deck collect?

None. There is no account, no analytics and no cloud. Commands travel from your iPhone straight to your Mac. The one thing Deck does over the internet: the Mac app asks GitHub once a day whether a newer version exists.
