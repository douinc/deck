# Deck launch film: storylines

30 s promo for the whole app (Mac + iPhone + Apple Watch). 1920×1080, 30 fps, music and a few effects, no voiceover.

**The one thing:** your iPhone and Apple Watch already are the presentation remote.
**What the viewer remembers:** "I don't need to buy or carry a clicker."
**Sources:** README.md, `assets/store-preview-images/`, `public/screenshot/`, `public/demo.MP4`, `deck-logo.png`. Every line of copy below is from those.

## A. "Next" (recommended)

- **Device / form:** the feature as the hook / object-to-object chain. The film is itself a presentation, and every cut is a slide advance caused by a real Deck input shown on screen.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a thumb presses Next and the whole frame advances like a slide; then a double tap on the watch advances it again, then a wrist flick, then the pointer. They feel the remote working on the film they are watching, and the app's own timer counts the film's real runtime from 00:00 to 00:30.
- **First two seconds:** the real iPhone Next button fills the right half of the frame on near-black, timer reading 00:00. At 0.4 s a thumb-press ripple lands on it; on that frame the left half slides away like a slide change and the title "Your iPhone is the clicker" is the new slide.
- **Beats:** hook (tap Next, frame advances) → tension (slide: "$1000 iPhone. $2000 Mac. And a $30–$100 clicker?") → reveal (Deck named) → proof as verbs: tap (iPhone), double tap (Watch), flick (Watch), point (pointer mode), keep time (timer + Live Activity) → payoff (the real demo footage: a wrist flick changes the slide on the wall screen) → claim ("No dongles, no BS.") → end card (the three logo cards deal in like slides, "Deck", App Store + `brew install --cask deck`).
- **Look:** near-black ground with full-bleed brand-blue slides, heavy white sans from the store previews, real device screens at 1.6–2× and nothing floating small.
- **Sound:** tight minimal pulse around 120 bpm; the bed drops out for the demo footage and returns on the claim; one click per Deck input, one whoosh per slide advance (two takes rotated).

## B. "The clicker tax"

- **Device / form:** life before and after / type carries the argument, cut on the beat.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** giant type does the arithmetic from the README: a $1000 iPhone, a $2000 Mac, and a $30–$100 clicker with a dongle and a dead battery. The clicker line is struck through and the phone already in their hand takes its place.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is "$1000" in white filling the frame on blue; at 0.6 s it hard-cuts to "$2000"; at 1.3 s to "+ $30–$100?" with the last figure small and red.
- **Beats:** hook (three prices) → tension ("for a clicker?") → reveal (strike-through, "Deck") → proof (pair in a tap, big buttons, wrist, timer; one colour field each) → payoff ("Works with any app that uses arrow keys": Keynote, PowerPoint, Google Slides, Figma) → claim ("No cloud. No accounts. No internet needed.") → end card.
- **Look:** alternating blue and near-black colour fields, type at 300 px and up, the product appears only as tight crops of real screens.
- **Sound:** percussive beat around 128 bpm with every cut on a hit; near-silence under the strike-through, then the full track.

## C. "Hands free" (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** the result first / an endless zoom.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** it opens on the real footage, full frame: a hand flicks and the slide on the wall changes, with nothing in the hand. Then the camera dives into the watch on that wrist, through the iPhone, into the Mac menu bar, and out onto the slide, showing the chain that made it happen.
- **First two seconds:** `demo.MP4` playing at normal speed in a tall centred frame on a blurred extension of itself; at 1.2 s the wrist flicks and the wall slide changes; "Nothing in your hand" lands on that frame.
- **Beats:** hook (the flick) → tension (replay, slowed: "how?") → reveal (zoom into the watch face: Deck) → proof (Watch → iPhone → Mac → Keynote, each the inside of the last) → payoff (pull all the way back out to the room, flick again) → claim ("Present from your wrist.") → end card.
- **Look:** the footage's warm office light against brand blue, device screens entered through their own glass.
- **Sound:** room tone only under the first flick, then a slow build that peaks on the pull-back; one riser, on the payoff.
